Label convention
  Serving-cell Event A2 with a speed-based target tier.
  Maintain when RSRP >= -100 dBm, SNR >= -5 dB and delta_RSRP > -2 dB.
  Otherwise Speed >= 30 km/h selects a macro handover, and a lower speed
  selects a small-cell handover. The decision is held for 2.0 s.
  delta_RSRP(t) = RSRP(t) - RSRP(t-1) inside a session when the step is <= 2 s.
  The Table 1 sites are stored for the later network simulation.
  Their path loss is PL = A_ref + 10 * alpha * log10(d / 1 m), d >= 10 m.
